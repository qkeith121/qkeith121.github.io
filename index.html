About

Hello, I'm Keith. you can call me Kifu 「キーフ」. I'm currently studying Japanese Language and Japanese Literature【日本語と日本の文学「Nihongo　Bungaku」】 Working really hard to reach N2~N1 Proficiency for JLPT. Other than that, I'm fascinated by web development, game programming and technology in general.

During my free time, I love exploring indie games, mangas and light novels.

Be yourself, and always enjoy what you do!~
